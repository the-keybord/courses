# Courses Hub

Welcome to the **Courses Repository**! This repository contains comprehensive curricula, lesson plans, presentations, and interactive activities designed for kids and teens across various educational domains.

---

## 📚 Available Courses

### 1. [3D School Junior](3d-school-junior/README.md)
- **Target Age Group**: **8–10 years old**
- **Domain**: 3D Discovery & Kids Encyclopedia (Fun Facts, Simple 3D Shapes, Tinkercad Basics)
- **Structure**: Independent Encyclopedia & Discovery themes (120 min per lesson)
- **Master File**: [`3d-school-junior/README.md`](3d-school-junior/README.md)

### 2. [3D School Start](3d-school-start/README.md)
- **Target Age Group**: **10–12 years old**
- **Domain**: 3D Modeling & Printing Basics (FDM, Tinkercad, Spatial Thinking)
- **Structure**: 4 Major Modules (120 min per lesson)
- **Master File**: [`3d-school-start/README.md`](3d-school-start/README.md)

### 3. [3D School Pro](3d-school-pro/README.md)
- **Target Age Group**: **12–14 years old**
- **Domain**: Advanced 3D Modeling, Engineering, Technical Specs & Slicing
- **Structure**: 4 Major Modules (120 min per lesson)
- **Master File**: [`3d-school-pro/README.md`](3d-school-pro/README.md)

### 4. [Robot Factory: Evolution](robot-factory-evolution/README.md)
- **Target Age Group**: **11–15 years old** (Level 2 / Year 2)
- **Domain**: Advanced Robotics, ESP32, BLE Control, Smart Sensors, 3D CAD & Orbit Odyssey Challenge
- **Structure**: Free relate format with practical challenges, team projects, and competitive arenas (120 min per lesson)
- **Master File**: [`robot-factory-evolution/README.md`](robot-factory-evolution/README.md)
